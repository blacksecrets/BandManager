Band Roles

What each band member does - Lead Guitar, Bass, Sound Engineer, and so on. A member can hold more than one role, and more than one member can hold the same role. Pick a member, then check off their role(s), then save.

Acts

A band's performance configurations - most bands have just one, but a second, very different one (e.g. a full-electric show vs. an acoustic/unplugged show) gets its own gear list, stage plot, and Tech Rider entirely.
