Pedal & keyboard controls

Pair your AirTurn (or any Bluetooth page-turner) in your device's OS Bluetooth settings, the same way you'd pair a wireless keyboard - no setup needed in BandManager+ itself. Once paired, this page reads whatever key the pedal sends.

  • Next section / song - Right arrow, Down arrow, Page Down, or Space
  • Previous section / song - Left arrow, Up arrow, or Page Up
  • Jump to a song - open the song list (☰) and click one

A song's lyrics can be split into pedal-advance sections by leaving a blank line between them, back on its Repertoire detail page.
