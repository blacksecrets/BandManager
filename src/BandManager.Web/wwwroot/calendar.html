Calendar

Select a band from the switcher above to see its calendar.

Sync to your calendar

Subscribe once and this calendar's gigs and rehearsals stay up to date in your own Google, Apple, or Outlook calendar - or export a snapshot file to import manually.

Google Calendar

Other calendars → + → From URL, then paste the feed URL above.

Outlook

Add calendar → Subscribe from web, then paste the feed URL above.

Apple Calendar

File → New Calendar Subscription, then paste the feed URL above. Apple refreshes subscribed calendars on its own schedule - often a few hours, not instantly - so updates here may lag behind Google/Outlook a bit. That's a limit of Apple's own subscription feature, not something BandManager controls.
